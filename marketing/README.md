## Positioning

**Free, open source, and completely local**

**Capture the whole page. Make it yours.**

For people sharing feedback, documenting a page, or explaining a bug, Scrollout combines full-page capture and a local editor. Every editing tool and export is included. Screenshots never upload to FarFlash.

Use these audience angles:

- Developers and QA: show the page state, add a precise arrow, and attach reproduction steps. Link to the bug-report guide.
- Designers and teams: capture a long layout, crop to the relevant section, and annotate the decision. Use the actual editor screenshots.
- Students and researchers: preserve visual context alongside source URLs and citation details. Do not describe screenshots as searchable or accessible text documents.
- Open-source users: MIT source, local capture/edit/export, no account or paid upgrade, four declared Chrome permissions.

## Approved factual claims

- Desktop Chrome 120+; Manifest V3.
- Full document capture across both axes, bounded by the initial dimensions.
- Draw, highlight, arrows, shapes, text, crop, opaque black redaction, move/resize/delete, undo/redo, zoom.
- PNG, JPEG at 92% quality, paginated A4 PDF, PNG clipboard. Oversized image exports split into numbered ZIP parts.
- Screenshots and edits are processed locally. FarFlash does not receive captures through the extension.
- No account, backend, analytics, subscription, screenshot upload service, or paywall.
- Temporary local captures; export before closing the last editor. Browser restarts expire sessions.
- MIT license. Inter is bundled under its own SIL Open Font License.

Do not claim that all websites work, that an editable session is permanently redacted, that every PDF is searchable, that files are forensically erased, that an unreleased version has passed Chrome Web Store review, or that QA is an independent security audit. No fabricated ratings, reviews, user counts, awards, benchmarks, or endorsements.
