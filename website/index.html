A few short answers.

Simple tools, clear boundaries.

Is every tool really free?

Yes. All capture, editing, redaction, and export tools are included. There is no subscription, account, or paid upgrade.

Does it work on every page?

Scrollout captures ordinary document scrolling in desktop Chrome 120 and later. Chrome-owned pages, the Chrome Web Store, and other protected pages can restrict capture. On app pages where the document does not scroll, version 1.0.4 and later can capture a large, fully visible main scrolling panel. Small widgets, other panels, and embedded frames keep their visible contents. Infinite feeds stop at the initial page length.

What happens to my screenshots?

They stay in temporary local extension storage. Reloading the editor preserves its saved edits. Closing or leaving the last editor for a capture deletes that capture. Restarting Chrome expires temporary sessions. Files you export and clipboard images are separate and remain under your control.
