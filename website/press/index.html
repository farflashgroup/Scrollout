About Scrollout

Scrollout is a free Chrome extension from FarFlash Group Ltd. Capture an entire webpage, then draw, highlight, crop, add arrows, shapes or text, redact sensitive areas, and export PNG, JPEG, or paginated A4 PDF. Screenshots are processed locally and never uploaded to FarFlash. There are no accounts, analytics, subscriptions, or paid editing tools.

Use the assets accurately

Keep the logo’s shape and colors intact, with clear space of at least one-quarter of the symbol’s width. Editor screenshots use illustrative Fieldnotes content. Describe them as product examples; they are not customer testimonials. Inclusion here does not imply that Scrollout endorses a publication or another product.

Try it and check the boundaries

Install from the Chrome Web Store. Current installation instructions and ZIP download are kept on the homepage. Capture covers ordinary document scrolling at the initial page length. Nested scrolling widgets and embedded frames retain their visible contents. Browser-protected pages can block capture. Captures are temporary; export before closing the last editor.
