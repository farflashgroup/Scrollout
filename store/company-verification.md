# Company publisher verification handoff

FarFlash Group Ltd. is releasing Scrollout as a company product. The account owner selected Trader and explicitly authorized the industry marketplace agreement. Developer registration is already paid. Company verification remains incomplete; no extension draft has been uploaded or submitted.

Use a Google payments profile whose account type is **Business / Organization**, with the exact registered company name and address. The saved profile shown during setup was labelled **Individual**; giving an Individual profile a company name does not make it an organization profile. The current verification screen requests personal ID and proof of name/address; those requests alone do not establish the account type, since Google can also verify an organization's representative.

Return to the payments profile picker and select an existing organization profile or choose **Create new payments profile**, then the business/organization type. The account owner supplies the company's registration details and any requested representative ID or documents directly to Google. Do not enter a personal name in place of the company's legal identity.

If the individual verification flow cannot be changed from its picker, Google's Chrome Web Store FAQ documents restarting verification by changing Trader to Non-Trader and back to Trader. This is a restart procedure, not a new determination of publisher status; do not leave the company declared as Non-Trader. The account owner should perform that declaration change if needed.

After verification permits account editing, set the public publisher display name to **FarFlash Group Ltd.** and verify **scrollout@farflash.com** as the public contact email. These fields are still pending. Trader legal name, address, and contact phone are displayed publicly on extension listings. Uploading identity documents is not necessary for the prepared local extension to run.
