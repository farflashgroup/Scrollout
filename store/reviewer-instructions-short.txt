Scrollout 1.0.4. No login or payment. Pin it, open a long HTTPS page, and click its toolbar icon; keep the source active. Draw, crop, redact, undo/redo, and export PNG/JPEG/PDF or Copy image. Click again or press Escape to cancel. New: main app-panel capture, including virtualized rows, when the document does not scroll; stable editor fitting. Captures stay local and expire when the last editor closes. Help has optional release checks. Source/tests: github.com/farflashgroup/Scrollout
