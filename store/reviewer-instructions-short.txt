No login, payment, or setup required. Pin Scrollout, open a normal long HTTPS page, and click its toolbar icon. Keep the page active until the editor opens. Draw, highlight, add shapes/text, crop, redact, undo/redo, then export PNG/JPEG/PDF or Copy image. Click the icon again or press Escape to cancel capture. Chrome 120+. Captures stay local and are deleted when the last editor closes. Details: https://github.com/farflashgroup/Scrollout
